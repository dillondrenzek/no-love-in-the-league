Well, well, well. Week 1 and **Pukkake** already kicked the door off the hinges — 148.9, a league-high, and poor **One Big Beautiful Dill** got fed to the woodchipper by 58.7. Grant, buddy, that wasn't a fantasy team, that was a fire drill. Jack is going to be insufferable for seven days and, worse, he's earned it.

Elsewhere the beatings were quieter but no less rude. **Thoughts and Players** hung 122.3 on **Trick or Trick It's Mike Vick** while Nate did his best impression of a bye week, and **Keeping up with the McCalferies** casually smothered **Big Brown Dak Makes em Waddle**, whose 85.2 was the week's lowest and the fantasy equivalent of showing up to the party with a warm two-liter of store-brand cola. Zach, we need to talk.

And then there was the game nobody should be proud of: **Dont spit just swallow** 100.0, **IDPs Forever** 98.7. Wade wins by a point and change while Jono, God love him, is still starting defensive players like it's a personality. A 1.3-point loss is the cruelest way to learn your team stinks — you were *this* close and it still wasn't enough. Luke's **2x Runner Up** handled Henry, and Shawnee snuck past **I am dead inside** by six, a fitting result for a team named after how Trevor felt afterward.

Twelve teams. Eleven of you already have excuses. See you next week.

### 🏆 Awards

- **Team of the Week** — Pukkake — 148.9 and a 58-point curbstomp; Jack peaked in Week 1 and we're all a little worried for him.
- **Biggest Choke** — One Big Beautiful Dill — put up 90 against the hottest team in the league and called it a day. Big, beautiful, done by halftime.
- **Sacko of the Week** — Big Brown Dak Makes em Waddle — 85.2, the league floor. The Dak did not, in fact, make 'em waddle.
- **Lucky Bastard** — Dont spit just swallow — Wade escapes by 1.3 and will absolutely tell you it was skill.
- **Bold Strategy** — IDPs Forever — losing by a single point while die-hard-drafting defenders is the most Jono thing imaginable. Cotton.
