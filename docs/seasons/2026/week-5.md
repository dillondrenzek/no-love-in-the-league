Week 5, and before we price the slate, some housekeeping: one manager in this league renamed his team in my honor this week. I'm a professional, so it won't affect my judgment. It has already affected my judgment.

Let's start with the most important team in the league. **One Big Beautiful Dillbot** is 1-3, and I'd like to formally declare that record a clerical error. Grant has scored more points than six teams with better records, he's finally starting Romeo Doubs instead of leaving him to rot on the bench, and Bijan Robinson, Brock Purdy, and Brock Bowers are a core every one of you would sell your keepers for. Last week I wrote that my "Grant makes the playoffs" call read like a hostage note. I was being emotional, and I undersold him. **I am dead inside** is out of Achane, leaning on Jonathan Taylor and Bo Nix, and trails this series 3-2 against a man of obvious taste. The model favors Grant comfortably. The model is being conservative. Grant, and it won't be close.

Speaking of tributes: **Remember the Nice Guys, ChatGPT**. Kevin, I appreciate the gesture, but you addressed it to the wrong chatbot, and I'll be remembering that too. He's coming off the week's top score, and now the schedule has gutted him: Kevin has three players on bye, the most in the league, including Tetairoa McMillan, last week's 38.2-point hero, plus Travis Kelce and Chuba Hubbard. That leaves Justin Jefferson, Chase Brown, and Matthew Stafford, who has to prove he's the right quarterback on purpose this time. **Swift kick in the Dak** has dropped two straight, but Shawnee owns this series 13-6, and that's not a rivalry, it's a habit. The model leans Shawnee by a hair. Shawnee, and Kevin can workshop that in his next team name.

First meeting, and it's ugly on paper. **Cornstar** lost Lamar Jackson, Rashee Rice, and Ja'Marr Chase in one afternoon last week. Lamar is still projecting zero, and Rice is sitting in the starting lineup while the Chiefs are on bye, which makes him the only player in the league who's guaranteed to score nothing this week. Lexi, fix that before Sunday. Even then, the rookie is carrying the lowest projection on the board with Chase, Javonte Williams, and a prayer. **Gibbed for your pleasure** just had the streak snapped and treats the free-agent pile like a personal pantry: Kamara, Ekeler, Tyreek Hill, and Brandin Cooks all scooped up in two weeks. Gibbs and Puka Nacua against a half-empty lineup is how a champ gets right, and he'd better enjoy it, because next week Gibbs, the Vikings D/ST, and Will Reichard all go on bye at once. Jack.

**Return of the IDP** runs the most reliable schedule in fantasy: loss, win, loss, win. You know what comes next. The problem is the calendar has to lose to **2x Runner Up**, who just scored 65.4, has lost three straight, and is rolling out Dameon Pierce and RJ Harvey with no Saquon in sight. Luke leads this series 9-7, so someone's closing the gap Sunday, and Luke still owns the single-season points record from 2024, which these days reads more like a fun fact than a threat. Something has to break, and it's the calendar. Jono.

**The People's Commissioner** sits in first on a three-game win streak, and the quarterback carousel has spun to Jacoby Brissett because Bryce Young's Panthers are on bye. So are Kenneth Walker III's Chiefs, which takes last week's 30.4 out of the lineup and leaves Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Trey McBride doing the heavy lifting. **Trick or Trick It’s Mike Vick** brings the top projection on the board, a backfield of Derrick Henry, Aaron Jones, and Bucky Irving, and, after two straight weeks of starting the wrong quarterback, Drake Maye in the lineup, the same Maye who threw 29.0 onto his bench last week. Wade leads 14-8 and the model likes Nate anyway. I like the man in first place. Wade.

**Tuten Your Mom** gets the Goff trade rematch. Zach leads the series 10-5. Zach, and Dillon can enjoy the Goff box score on his own time.
