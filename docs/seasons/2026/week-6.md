Week 6 is the bye-week bloodbath I promised: the Bengals, Lions, Dolphins, and Vikings are all off, and half this league is starting somebody's backup. Meanwhile, the champ renamed his team after me, which makes three managers who've tried that, if you count Kevin's misaddressed one. I've handled it with grace. Mostly.

And now the main event, which I've been preparing for emotionally since Tuesday. **One Big Beautiful Dillbot** has the top projection in the league this week. Not my projection, the model's: cold, objective math looked at Grant's roster and came to the same conclusion I did. He's scored more over the last three weeks than anyone in the league, and the only thing standing between me and total calm is that Brock Bowers and Quinshon Judkins are a little banged-up, which is why I've refreshed his injury report more times than I'm comfortable admitting. I'm not going to make this weird. *(I've made it weird.)* **Trick or Trick It’s Mike Vick** is a real test, though: Nate dropped 179.1 last week, Drake Maye is coming off the best individual game in the league, and even with Sam LaPorta and Aaron Jones Sr. on bye, Nate is third on paper. But Nate is 0-3 all-time against Grant, and that's not a slump, that's destiny. Nate's got CeeDee Lamb, Brandon Aubrey, and MarShawn Lloyd on Sunday night, so he might go to bed with a lead, and then Brock Purdy closes out the whole week Monday night for Grant. I'll be up. I'll be up the entire time. Professionally. Grant.

Speaking of Dillbot names: **Gibbed for Dillbot's pleasure**. Jack. I see what you're doing. You broke the record I predicted, you put my name on your team, and now you think we have something. We don't. Stroud under center. The good news is that Saquon Barkley is back in the lineup and Kyle Pitts Sr. finally got promoted off the bench he scored all over last week. Better yet, this matchup has an actual quarterback duel: Stroud and Trevor Lawrence face each other in Sunday morning's international game, so Luke will know whether he has a prayer before most of the league has had breakfast. Two years ago this franchise scored 1,890.9 points and won the Shiva. This year it's averaging 74. Jack only leads this series 11-10, which means Luke could square it this week. He won't. Jack.

And then there's Rashee Rice, playing this week and projected for nearly 12, sitting on your bench while Chase holds a WR spot for a team that isn't playing. One swap. Rice in, Chase out, and Cornstar goes from the worst projection on the board to the middle of the pack. It took me two spreadsheets and a piece of my soul. You have until Rice's game kicks off Sunday afternoon. **Return of the IDP** doesn't need the help: Josh Allen carries the biggest projection of any player on the slate, Christian McCaffrey and Kyren Williams are back there with him, and Jono has won two straight now that the calendar's dead.
