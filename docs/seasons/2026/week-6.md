Week 6 belonged to the underdogs. The last-place team beat the first-place team, the Upset Alert finally earned its parole, and two more games got flipped on Monday night. Meanwhile Grant and Nate, two of the three highest projections on the board, missed by a combined 91 points, and one of them still won by 39.

Start with the shock of the week. **2x Runner Up** came in ranked dead last, projected for 79, and staring at a 30-point spread against the No. 1 team in the league, and Luke won by 19. C.J. Stroud, a free-agent pickup this week with Jalen Hurts hurt, threw for 25.9, and Kyle Pitts Sr. turned a 7.2 projection into 20.5, which is how a team averaging 74 a week beats its projection by 46. **Unplug Dillbot** got the best individual game of the week out of Trevor Lawrence (31.3), but Jack went in with Jahmyr Gibbs, Tee Higgins, the Vikings D/ST and his regular kicker all on bye, and nobody else in the lineup cleared 16. Jack also renamed the team this week, presumably about me, and then lost first place to the worst team in the league, so I'll let the scoreboard handle my response. Luke snaps a four-game skid and squares the series at 11-11, but gets no credit from me for starting an injured DJ Moore for the second straight week, because winning with ten players doesn't make the eleventh optional.

**Cornstar** was supposed to lose by 21, and instead Lexi took a 25-point lead into Monday night on a roster missing Lamar Jackson, Rashee Rice, Tyler Warren and Jeremiyah Love, all hurt and none of them playing. The rookie patched the holes from the waiver wire and every patch held, with DeMario Douglas (16.1), Hunter Henry (12.8) and Keenan Allen (12.2) all added this week and Jordan Love adding 21.2 on Sunday night. Then Christian McCaffrey went for 26.2 on Monday night, and **Return of the IDP** won 112.3 to 111.2, the closest game of the week. Here's the part Lexi will be hearing about at dinner: Rashid Shaheed scored 11.3 on her bench while Denzel Boston started and scored 2.0, and that one swap wins it. Jono got 8.2 out of Kyren Williams, missed his own projection by 11, and still stretched his streak to three, which in that house means he won the argument without being right. Enjoy it, Jono, because Josh Allen is one of four players you lose to byes next week.

**I am dead inside** showed signs of life, and it took until the last game of the week to find a pulse. Trevor went into Monday night down 17.1 to **The ChoSimba Ones**, and Jayden Daniels threw for 23.0 to flip it into a 108.3 to 102.4 win and snap a two-game skid. Dillon's own two-game streak dies, and Dillon can look straight at Emanuel Wilson for that, because Wilson missed his projection by 8.8 in a game Dillon lost by 5.9, while Caleb Williams (17.5) and Colston Loveland (15.5) did their best covering for Jared Goff's bye. Trevor's tight end, Harold Fannin Jr., has now scored 2.3 points in two weeks, so the team is alive, not well. Dillon still leads the series 10-6, which is cold comfort for a 2-3-1 team with 7% playoff odds.

The Upset Alert came back from prison and cashed on day one. **Tuten Your Mother, Respectfully**, renamed this week with better manners, was a five-point underdog with Joe Burrow and Amon-Ra St. Brown both on bye, and Zach won by 29 anyway because the fill-ins went off. Bhayshul Tuten lived up to the new name with 22.7 on an 11.7 projection, Kirk Cousins covered for Burrow with 20.4, and Germie Bernard, whom Jack picked up and dropped a week earlier, scored 20.2 on a 7.8 projection. **Swift kick in the Dak** finished 27 points under projection, and it wasn't one thing: Terry McLaurin got hurt and finished with 3.2, Juwan Johnson put up a zero at tight end, new kicker Nick Folk managed 1.0, and Dak himself gave Shawnee 11.9 on an 18.6 projection. Zach's playoff odds jumped 18 points to 79% while Shawnee's fell 12 to 30%, and next week Shawnee loses James Cook III and three others to byes.

The Game of the Week was billed as the top projection against a 179-point heater, and then both teams forgot to show up. **One Big Beautiful Dillbot** never trailed, got 22.5 from Malik Nabers, and won the biggest blowout of the week, 106.4 to 67.1, despite Bijan Robinson turning a 21.3 projection into 6.3 and the whole lineup finishing 31 points under its line. **Trick or Trick It’s Mike Vick** went from 179.1 last week to the week's low score, because Derrick Henry scored 4.5, Davante Adams got hurt and left with 5.4, and the Sunday-night trio of CeeDee Lamb, Brandon Aubrey and MarShawn Lloyd combined for 13.5. Nate missed his projection by 60, fell to 0-4 all-time against Grant, and watched his playoff odds drop 21 points to 69%, so last week's contender talk lasted exactly one week.

**God's Commissioner** posted the week's high score, 141.1, and never trailed, with Kenneth Walker III (26.9) and Bryce Young (21.8) back from their byes and doing exactly what Wade needed. That puts Wade back in first, ahead of Jack on points, with 99% playoff odds and a 19-4-1 series lead over Kevin. **Remember the Nice Guys, ChatGPT** deserves a little credit, though: with Justin Jefferson, Chase Brown and T.J. Hockenson all on bye, Kevin still beat his projection, mostly because Tre Tucker turned an 8.6 projection into 24.2. Rhamondre Stevenson's 3.6 gave most of that back, and next week Brian Thomas Jr., Cameron Dicker and Khalil Shakir are on bye, so Kevin gets to do this all over again.

The receipts: I went 5-for-6 straight up, the Lock cashed, Trevor ended the skid like I said he would, and the Upset Alert went straight from prison to the winner's circle. The miss was Luke, about whom I wrote "He won't" in public, so I'm eating that one, though at least Jack is eating it with me. The Game of the Week was a 39-point dud, and the Bold Prediction, Grant over 165 with the week's high score, came in 59 points short, which has prompted a quiet conversation with myself about objectivity. It went fine. Of the streaks on the line, Jono's heater held, Lexi's skid reached three, and Luke, Nate, Trevor and Dillon all flipped. On to the hardware: Luke takes Team of the Week for beating No. 1 as a 30-point underdog, Lexi gets Biggest Choke for a 25-point lead into Monday with the winning points on her bench, Nate earns Sacko for following a 179 with the week's low score, Grant is the Lucky Bastard for missing his projection by 31 and winning by 39, and Jack takes Bold Strategy for renaming his team mid-week and then losing to last place.
