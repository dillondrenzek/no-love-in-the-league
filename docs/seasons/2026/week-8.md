Houston, New Orleans, the Giants and San Francisco are all off this week, which takes Christian McCaffrey, George Kittle, Malik Nabers, Nico Collins and C.J. Stroud out of the league at once. Meanwhile the commish is one win from history, and Dillon is about to see the reigning champ two weeks in a row.

**God's Commissioner** is first in the standings, first in the projections, and sitting on 99 career regular-season wins, a number no manager in this league has ever reached. Jack is next on the list at 93, so the question isn't really who gets to 100 first, it's when, and Wade would like it to be Sunday. He fired Bryce Young after last week's 6.3 and handed the job to Geno Smith, a free-agent pickup this week, and his new Commanders D/ST plays Sunday night against Saquon Barkley, DeVonta Smith and the Eagles D/ST, which is Luke's entire Sunday night. **2x Runner Up** has the lowest projection in the league, Stroud on bye, and Michael Penix Jr. at quarterback, the same Penix that Trevor picked up last week and threw back. Luke has beaten Wade 11 times in 16 meetings, which is a fun fact and nothing more for a 2-5 team averaging 81 points. Wade, and somebody order the cake.

Part one of the double header: **Gibbed for your pleasure** and **The ChoSimba Ones** play this week and next week, and Jack leads the series 10-8, so by the end of Week 9 it's either tied or the champ's up four. Jack gets Trevor Lawrence back from his bye to go with Jahmyr Gibbs, Puka Nacua and Parker Washington, and with eight starters in the Sunday early games, he'll know how this went before the late games kick off, even without Kittle and Travis Etienne Jr. Dillon is 2-4-1 on a two-game skid, Woody Marks is on bye one week after Dillon traded for him, and Caleb Williams is finally starting, one week after scoring 24 on the bench. Caleb and Colston Loveland are Dillon's whole Monday night in Seattle, so if this is close, the Bears decide it. And if it isn't, Dillon gets a rematch next week without DK Metcalf, who's on bye. Jack.

**Remember the Nice Guys, ChatGPT** and **Trick or Trick It’s Mike Vick** have exactly 66 career wins each, and they're the only two managers in league history with 100 losses: Kevin has exactly 100, Nate has 102. So while Wade chases 100 wins, the other 100 club holds its meeting, and with the series tied 8-8, one of them leaves with the lead. Kevin has three starters Thursday night, the most of anyone, with Chuba Hubbard, Tetairoa McMillan and Josh Jacobs all in Carolina at Green Bay, so his three-game skid gets an early verdict. Nate has Drake Maye, the top-projected starter in the league, plus a Derrick Henry who owes him for two straight duds, and the projection likes Nate. Nate.

Josh Allen is back from his bye for **Return of the IDP**, which is good timing, because McCaffrey's now taking his turn. **I am dead inside** gets Jayden Daniels back for Sunday night, has won two straight, beat its projection by 16 last week, and is favored, with Omar Cooper Jr. back in the lineup after his 20-point debut. Jono's coming off a 45-point loss to Wade and needs to bank this one, because next week Carnell Tate, Pat Freiermuth and the Titans D/ST are all on bye, the most in the league, so brace yourself, Jono. Over in the other half of the household, Jono and Lexi are both underdogs this week and projected within a tenth of a point of each other, so the bragging rights in that house are a coin flip before kickoff. Jono.

The two hottest teams in the standings meet in **Tuten Your Mother, Respectfully** against **One Big Beautiful Dillbot**: Zach has won two straight, and Grant has won three, the longest active win streak in the league. Zach gets Bhayshul Tuten back on the team that bears his name, and loses Alvin Kamara, who arrived by trade last week and promptly went on bye, along with Cam Skattebo and Isaiah Likely. Grant has three on bye too, Nabers, Brock Purdy and his kicker, so Patrick Mahomes starts at quarterback and the kicker is Chase McLaughlin, whom Zach picked up last week and let go. Grant's whole lineup is done by Sunday afternoon, while Zach has Chig Okonkwo on Sunday night and Luther Burden III on Monday night, and the projection likes Zach. In keeping with my new professional standards, I'm picking against Grant, and I'd like the record to show it hurt. Zach.

**Cornstar** and **Swift kick in the Dak** have never played, and somebody's skid ends here: the rookie has lost four straight, and the reigning sacko has lost two. Lexi opens Thursday night with Jordan Love and the Panthers D/ST, who are playing each other, so whatever happens in Green Bay, she gets some of it back. Shawnee's missing Nico Collins and David Montgomery to byes, is starting two waiver claims from this week, Brenton Strange at tight end and Antonio Williams on Sunday night, and finishes with D'Andre Swift on Monday night. Lexi was my Upset Alert last week and lost by 30, and I remember. Shawnee.

So here's the card. **Jack vs Dillon** is the Game of the Week because it's half of a two-week series, and if Dillon doesn't take one of the two, his season is over before the Halloween candy runs out. The Lock is **Wade**, because the top projection against the bottom one with a milestone on the line is as safe as this slate gets. The Upset Alert is **Jono**, because Josh Allen is back and Trevor's heater is two games long. And the Bold Prediction keeps the theme: Wade gets to 100 wins with the week's high score, his second straight, and Kevin gets to 101 losses the same weekend.
