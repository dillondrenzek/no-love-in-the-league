Week 1 handed us a tie, a 166-burger, and a rookie who barely escaped her first test — now the overreactions harden into Week 2. One week of data, twelve fragile egos, and my card. Let's price it.

The champ isn't cooling off. **Gibbed for your pleasure** rode Gibbs and Puka Nacua to the best score in the league last week, and the model makes Jack a comfortable favorite over **One Big Beautiful Dill** — which tracks, because Jack has _never_ lost to Grant, 3-0 all-time. Grant's got Brock Purdy and Bijan Robinson to build a case, but "0-for-his-career against this guy" is a nasty headwind, and he's dragging a Week 1 loss in behind him. I'll pick against the champ when I see a reason to; this isn't the week.

Here's the one I've got circled for pure comedy: the veteran who _tied_ his Week 1 matchup against the rookie who won hers. **Josh Jacobs Alibi** vs **Cornstar** grades out a dead coin flip, and Lexi already served notice on her debut — Lamar Jackson and Ja'Marr Chase headline a lineup that outscored most of the room. Kevin counters with Justin Jefferson and a deep bench, but a career .401 owner letting a first-timer breathe is exactly how the wrong kind of legend gets born. First meeting, no history to hide behind — I lean the rookie.

The model's got this one upside down. It likes **Trick or Trick It's Mike Vick** over **2x Runner Up**, but Luke owns the series 14-11 and Nate's a career .383 who just posted an 87 in a spot he was built to win. The talent's real — Derrick Henry and CeeDee Lamb will keep Nate breathing — but I am not fading a man who's beaten Nate fourteen times on the word of a single projection. Give me Luke.

Don't let the number lie to you on **The People's Commissioner** vs **I am dead inside**. Wade shows up dead last on paper this week, but that's eight names, not a lineup — he's got injured bodies in the training room and slots he hasn't filled yet. He'll claw most of that projection gap back by Sunday; the open question is whether a banged-up roster can claw back _all_ of it, and that may take some actual skill from him. Meanwhile, **I am dead inside** is the projection's darling — rich, for the guy who put up the league's lowest score in Week 1. Wade dropped 146 in a loss last week and Trevor mustered 85 in one of his own; if the Commissioner gets healthy and sets it right, he's got the players to beat the number and the matchup to drag Trevor to 0-2.

Circle this for real: the Nico Collins reunion. Shawnee shipped picks to pry Collins off **The ChoSimba Ones** back in the offseason, and now she gets to point him — plus Dak Prescott, James Cook, and D'Andre Swift — straight at Dillon. It's the closest rivalry on the board, Shawnee up 10-9 all-time, the reigning sacko is 1-0 and strutting, and Dillon's coming off a 39-point Caleb Williams eruption that only bought him a tie. Two rosters that hang points in bunches; the model calls it a toss-up and I won't argue.

Last one: **Tuten Your Mom** put up the second-most points in the league in Week 1, Ashton Jeanty cashing the trade the day it cleared, and Zach looks the part. The catch is **Return of the IDP** owns him 16-9 all-time — the kind of hex that laughs at one hot week — and Jono's got Josh Allen and Christian McCaffrey to keep it ugly. The model even leans Jono's way. Zach's the better team today; Jono's the worse matchup for him. I lean Jono, and I don't love it.

So here's the card. **Dillon–Shawnee** is the Game of the Week — a traded superstar aimed at his old team in the tightest rivalry we've got, that's the whole pitch. I'm locking the **champ**; 3-0 over Grant and fresh off the league's best number, so back Jack and don't overthink it. My upset is **Luke** — the model's in love with Nate, but a 14-11 series says it's reading the wrong week. And the bold one, write it down: Wade, starting eight and half a training room, still beats his projection and sends Trevor home 0-2.
