Week 7 had no drama, just damage. The closest game on the slate was decided by 23 points, the commish dropped 156 after I told him in writing how to fix his lineup, and both upsets landed on teams I picked to win.

Last week I pointed out, in public, that **God's Commissioner** had an empty lineup slot with Tony Pollard sitting on the bench, and then I picked against Wade because of it. Wade read the preview, put Pollard in the slot, and Pollard scored 35.0, the best individual game of the week, so the commish beat my pick with my own advice. Zay Flowers (23.7) and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (26.8 on Sunday night) piled on for the week's top score, 156.2, which was 43.6 over projection and plenty to bury Bryce Young's 6.3 at quarterback. **Return of the IDP** didn't play badly, with Jameson Williams turning an 8.9 projection into 23.6 and Aaron Rodgers scoring 14.0 against the man who waived him, more than double what Wade got from the quarterback he kept, but four players on bye was too many holes against a lineup that hot. Jono's three-game streak is over, the series is tied at 12-12-1, and Wade stays in first.

**The ChoSimba Ones** were favored by 9 and led by 21 after Thursday night and the early Sunday-morning game, and then lost by 38, which takes real commitment. **I am dead inside** scored 81 points in the Sunday early games alone, led by Omar Cooper Jr., a waiver claim this week who turned a 6.6 projection into 20.0, and Kirk Cousins, Zach's starter a week ago, closed it out with 22.4. Meanwhile Dillon finished 30.8 under projection, got 15.2 combined from DK Metcalf and Woody Marks in their first week after the trade, and left 51.0 on the bench, the most in the league, including 24.0 from Caleb Williams while Jared Goff started. That's Trevor over Dillon in back-to-back weeks, the series is down to 10-7, Trevor is on a two-game heater, and Dillon's playoff odds are 2%, which is less a number than a eulogy.

**Tuten Your Mother, Respectfully** played without Tuten, who was on bye, and didn't miss him, because the trade machine finally paid out. Zach sent DK Metcalf to Dillon for Luther Burden III and Alvin Kamara, Burden opened the week with 21.7 on Thursday night, and Joe Burrow came back from his bye with 32.8, so Zach never trailed and his playoff odds jumped 13 points to 92%. **Cornstar** started Tyler Allgeier, the running back she got from Zach for Metcalf, against Zach himself, and Allgeier answered with 6.4, which is the kind of revenge plot that ends with the villain getting a parking ticket. In fairness to the rookie, Metcalf only scored 6.6 for Dillon, so Lexi lost that trade by two-tenths of a point, which is more than Dillon can say about his. Javonte Williams scored 21.5 on Monday night after the game was long gone, the skid is now four, and with Jono also losing by 45, it was a quiet 0-2 Sunday in that house.

The Lock never sweated. **Gibbed for your pleasure** got 23.8 from Tee Higgins in his first game since the trade and 17.8 from Puka Nacua, and Jack won the biggest blowout of the week by 49.6 without much help from Jahmyr Gibbs (14.8 on a 23.5 projection). **Remember the Nice Guys, ChatGPT** actually led for a few hours on Sunday morning thanks to Chris Olave, and then Justin Jefferson scored 6.4 and the whole team finished 38.8 under projection. Kevin is now 3-18 all-time against Jack, the losing streak is at three, and his playoff odds dropped 14 points to 17%. As for Jack, he changed the name back last week and immediately started winning again, so it turns out the team just runs better on the original firmware.

**Trick or Trick It’s Mike Vick** never trailed, and that's the nicest thing I can say about it. Drake Maye opened with 29.4 on Thursday night and Bucky Irving went for 23.5, which covered for Derrick Henry scoring 5.4, his second dud in a row, and for Nate starting Christian Kirk for a zero after I told him in the preview that Kirk was on injured reserve. **Swift kick in the Dak** got exactly what the name promised, with D'Andre Swift scoring 17.0 on Thursday and Dak Prescott scoring 26.2 on Monday night, but everything in between fell through the floor, as Nico Collins (1.6) and Jalen Coker (1.4) combined for three points. Shawnee did listen and sat the banged-up David Montgomery, which I appreciate, but the playoff odds still fell 17 points to 13%, and next week Collins, Montgomery and two others are on bye.

Last week **2x Runner Up** beat the No. 1 team as a 30-point underdog, and I asked Luke to prove it wasn't a typo. It was a typo. **One Big Beautiful Dillbot** led after every window, Quinshon Judkins came back with 22.9 on an 11.9 projection, and Grant won by 42.9 even with Brock Purdy managing 12.9. Luke posted the week's low score, 73.0, and his entire Monday night was the Eagles, whose defense finished at -6.0 as Dallas hung 38 on it in a 17-point loss, and Jaylin Noel, a waiver claim this week, contributed 0.7. C.J. Stroud's 18.4 deserved better company, and Luke's Sacko odds are now 51%. Grant's streak is up to three, he leads the series 3-2, and his playoff odds rose 10 points to 91%, and that is all I will be saying about Grant, professionally.

The receipts: I went 3-for-6. The Lock cashed with the biggest blowout of the week, and Grant and Nate both came through. The Game of the Week was a pure self-own, because I diagnosed Wade's empty slot, he fixed it, and Pollard made me pay for it. The Upset Alert lost by 30, the Dillon pick lost by 38, and the Bold Prediction went half-right: Rodgers did outscore Bryce Young, 14.0 to 6.3, and Jono lost by 45 anyway. Grant's heater held, Jono's ended at three, and the skids for Kevin and Lexi reached three and four. On to the hardware: Wade takes Team of the Week for 156.2 and a 35-point running back he almost didn't start, Dillon gets Biggest Choke for turning a 21-point lead as the favorite into a 38-point loss, Luke takes Sacko for the low score and a negative defense on Monday night, Nate is the Lucky Bastard for winning by 23 with a zero from an injured receiver and 5.4 from Henry, and Lexi gets Bold Strategy for starting the player she traded for against the guy she traded with.
