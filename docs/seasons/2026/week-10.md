The top two projections in the league are separated by less than half a point, and one of those teams is starting a player who isn't going to play. **The People's Commissioner** has Mike Evans in the lineup, projected for zero, while Tony Pollard sits on the bench, and the last time I pointed out a hole like this, in Week 7, Wade fixed it, Pollard scored 35 and the commish beat my pick with my own advice. The quarterback situation is a love story: Caleb Williams is on bye, so Wade went back to Bryce Young, the quarterback he benched after a 6.3 in Week 7 and released a week later, which is the fantasy version of texting your ex because your new partner is out of town. **One Big Beautiful Dillbot** is coming off the week's top score with Brock Purdy, Bijan Robinson and Brock Bowers all healthy, and five of Grant's players are on bye next week, tied for the most in the league, so this is the week to bank a win. I'm picking Wade, because Wade reads this, Wade will fix the lineup, and I've been asked to keep my Grant coverage professional. Wade.
