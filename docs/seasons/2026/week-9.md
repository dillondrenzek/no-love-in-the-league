Week 9 gives us the most wins in league history against the most losses in league history, the reigning champ's second helping of Dillon, and a rookie starting a kicker on injured reserve. Pittsburgh and Tennessee are the only teams off, so the schedule has just one real victim this week, and it's the commish. Next week it's Luke, who should start preparing a statement.

Put this one in a museum: **God's Commissioner** has 100 career wins, **Trick or Trick It’s Mike Vick** has 103 career losses, and nobody in the history of this league has more of either. Wade celebrated the milestone by releasing Geno Smith one week after Geno's 20-point start, like a rented tux going back to the store, and dropped Bryce Young for good measure, so Caleb Williams finally comes out of the driveway as the only quarterback on the roster. That's a bold plan when your only quarterback is on bye next week, and it's bolder with Tony Pollard off after scoring 62 in two weeks, which is how the first-place team ends up fifth in the projections. Nate, last week's Biggest Choke, answers with a 91-point average over the last three weeks and Alvin Kamara, fresh off Zach's curb and headed straight to the bench. The model leans Wade and so do I, because nobody gets to 103 by accident. Wade.

Welcome to part two of the documentary. Part one ended 140.9 to 72.5, and **The ChoSimba Ones** prepared for the rematch by picking up Adonai Mitchell, a receiver Wade claimed off waivers this week and let go, which makes Wade less of a trade partner at this point than a supplier. Here's the part I can't stop thinking about: Dillon's last starter of the week is Colston Loveland on Sunday night, so Dillon's whole season-saving effort ends with Loveland catching passes from Caleb Williams, the quarterback Dillon traded to Wade, with every one of Caleb's points going to somebody else. DK Metcalf is on bye, which after last week's 0.9 is barely a demotion. **Gibbed for your pleasure** has the top projection in the league, gets George Kittle back, and has shown no interest in treating this as a contest. Dillon still has the best career winning percentage of any active manager, and is working very hard to fix that. Jack.

This is the only game on the slate between two teams with winning records, and they took very different roads to get here. **Tuten Your Mother, Respectfully** is 6-2 on Joe Burrow's 44.4 and a trade machine that has stopped making sense even to itself: Zach traded for Kamara in Week 7, got one game and 9.7 points out of him, sat through the bye, and dropped him, so the receipt is still warm and the player is on Nate's bench. **Return of the IDP** is 5-3 on a much simpler plan, which is having Josh Allen, and now Christian McCaffrey is back from his bye too. Zach has three starters tonight and Jono has none, while Allen closes the whole week on Monday night, so whatever lead Zach builds, Jono gets the last word on it. Zach is somehow 9-17 all-time against Jono, which won't decide a thing on Sunday, but I wanted Zach to have to read it. I'll take the team scoring 133 a game over the last three. Zach.

**Swift kick in the Dak** has David Montgomery in the starting lineup, and Montgomery is out, and I feel like I've written this before, because I have, in Week 7. This is Montgomery's third week on the roster and he hasn't scored a point for it, while Kaelon Black and Dylan Sampson sit on the bench with actual projections, so move one of them in, Shawnee. It might matter, because the reigning sacko nearly erased a 44-point deficit last week, and with D'Andre Swift on Sunday night and James Cook III on Monday night, the next comeback is already on the calendar. **One Big Beautiful Dillbot** gets Brock Purdy, Malik Nabers and the kicker back, has the third-best projection in the league, and at 4-4 is somehow 96% to make the playoffs, which I've been asked not to find attractive. Grant.

Last year these two finished second and third. This year **Remember the Nice Guys, ChatGPT** is 3-4-1 and **I am dead inside** is 3-5, and that team name has started reading less like a joke and more like a status report. Trevor's playoff odds are 4%, Drake London scored 1.3 last week, and the best decision of Trevor's whole season might be letting Michael Penix Jr. go one week before he scored -1.9 for somebody else. Kevin just ended a three-game skid by scoring 50 before most of the league had played a snap, is still sitting on exactly 100 career losses, and at 21% to make the playoffs finally has something to lose besides that number. Kevin.

The two lowest projections in the league meet in a rematch of Week 3, when **Cornstar** won by 60, and the rookie has one problem to solve first. Tyler Loop is in Lexi's starting lineup at kicker, Loop is on injured reserve, there's no other kicker on the roster, and Loop's game is tonight, so go get somebody off the wire, Lexi, because any working kicker at all makes you the favorite. And Jono, you're not even playing your wife this week, so you have no excuse for letting this one slide. **2x Runner Up** gets C.J. I picked Lexi in Week 7 and she lost by 30, then I faded her last week and she won, so the only move left is to pick her again and hope I've finally got the timing right. Lexi.
