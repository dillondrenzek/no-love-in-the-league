Week 4 closes out the first quarter of the season: the champ is alone at 3-0, four teams are bunched at 2-1 trying to catch him, and one former Shiva is still looking for his first win. Time to price it, and I've got a grudge to settle with my own Upset Alert.

Gibbs, Puka Nacua, and Trevor Lawrence lead the charge, with Alvin Kamara straight off the free-agent pile and into the lineup. Across the field, **The People's Commissioner** has won two straight, and Wade is starting Bryce Young again, a week after Jordan Love outscored him from the bench. Jack leads the series 8-5, so this has been a lopsided rivalry with Wade as the one taking the punches, and the model likes the champ. I don't. This is the week Jack finally tastes an L, and it's the commish who serves it.

**Tuten Your Mom** leads the league in total points and gets the cheapest date on the schedule. Burrow, Jeanty, Amon-Ra, and Skattebo are a nightmare on their own, and now Zach gets to start Josh Downs, the guy he pried out of Dillon's hands. **2x Runner Up** posted the week's low score, missed his projection by forty, and now sits dead last in points. Luke won the whole thing two years ago, and right now he looks a lot more like a Sacko than a Shiva. Zach leads the series 12-8 and the model makes him a heavy favorite. Zach, and it's not close.

First meeting, and it's the rookie who's favored, not the former champ. **Cornstar** just hung 134 on Luke and climbed three spots in my rankings behind Lamar Jackson and Ja'Marr Chase. **The ChoSimba Ones** are 0-2-1, the only winless team in the league, and coming off an 88. Dillon is a career .609 owner with a ring from 2018, and he's starting two tight ends and Devaughn Vele against a rookie who keeps winning by double digits. Lexi teaches the old man a lesson.

The model calls it dead even and the series sits 1-1, so somebody finally settles it. I'm riding the hot hand: Grant, and Kevin falls back to .500.

The problem is **I am dead inside**, which owns this series **8-1**. That isn't a rivalry, that's a landlord collecting rent. Trevor, and Nate can file the complaint with the league office.

And the bold one: Lamar Jackson outscores Dillon's entire starting receiver room.
