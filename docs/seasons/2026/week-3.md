Week 3 kicks off tonight, and the waiver wire went feral — half the league spent the week panic-shopping for quarterbacks, and the only two perfect records in The League share a mailing address. Jack and Shawnee are 2-0; everybody else is chasing the household. Let's price it.

**Gibbed for your pleasure** is the team to beat on paper — the top projection on the board, Puka Nacua back in the lineup, and 23 waiver adds already because the champ treats the wire like a buffet. The problem is who's across the table: **Trick or Trick It's Mike Vick** just hung the league-high score on Luke, and this series is as tight as they come, Jack up 9-8 all-time. Nate even grabbed Tyler Shough off the wire to sit behind Drake Maye, which is either savvy insurance or a QB controversy with a fuse on it. The model likes the champ; I would like if somebody finally takes control of this series. However, a 92-point "win" last week says the champ is more beatable than the standings let on.

Quietly, **Josh Jacobs Alibi** hasn't lost either — 1-0-1, third place, the only undefeated team in the league that isn't married to another one. Nobody's talking about it, which is exactly how a career .405 owner likes it. Kevin's QB room took a body blow too: Jaxson Dart, the guy who saved his Week 1 tie, is headed for season-ending surgery, so Kevin cut bait and fished Justin Herbert out of Wade's trash to sit behind Matthew Stafford. **I am dead inside** is 0-2 with Jayden Daniels also projecting zero, so it's the Bo Nix show, but Trevor owns this series 6-4 and the model calls it a coin flip that leans Kevin by a hair. I'm taking Trevor off the schneid.

First meeting: **2x Runner Up** vs **Cornstar**, and the model has the rookie favored. Luke is coming off the league's low score — a 77 with Saquon Barkley posting a 2 — and his big move this week was swapping Kyle Williams for Malik Washington. Lexi didn't make a single move: Lamar, Ja'Marr, lineup set, go live your life. I've hung my Upset Alert on Luke two weeks running and I'm not going back to that well. Rookie by a touchdown.

**One Big Beautiful Dill** is 0-2, dead last in the standings, and rostering Patrick Mahomes as a backup — the most expensive clipboard in fantasy. Grant starts Brock Purdy and the model makes him a slight favorite over **Return of the IDP**, but Jono's got Josh Allen, the top-projected player on the whole slate and fresh off a 43-point game. The series is 1-1, so somebody settles it Sunday. Until Grant proves he can set a lineup, I'm taking Jono.

**The People's Commissioner** looks like a powerhouse — the most points in the league — and acts like a man one bad Sunday from flipping the table. Wade dumped 45 on his own bench in Week 1, scraped together a win with half a training room in Week 2, and this week cut Justin Herbert, grabbed Bryce Young _and_ Jordan Love, and is starting Young while Love projects higher on the pine. Across the field, **The ChoSimba Ones** are a MASH unit: Caleb Williams, A.J. Brown, Jordan Mason, and Zach Charbonnet all projecting zeros, so Dillon flipped Josh Downs for Jared Goff, is rolling out two tight ends, and dropped **$133 of FAAB on Devaughn Vele**. Bold strategy, Cotton. Dillon owns Wade 9-5 all-time, but the model has Wade comfortably — so long as the commish doesn't outthink himself.

The reigning Sacko is 2-0 and the model _still_ has her dead last on paper — partly because **Swift kick in the Dak** currently has Nico Collins on the bench and Rashod Bateman in the lineup. Either Shawnee knows something or she hasn't set it yet. **Tuten Your Mom** is second in the league in points and leads the series 7-5, but Zach just shipped his backup QB, Jared Goff, to Dillon for Josh Downs, so it's Joe Burrow or bust. The model likes Zach by a lot. I like him by a little.

So here's the card. **Jack vs Nate** is the Game of the Week — the champ against last week's hottest team, in a series separated by a single game. The Lock is **Wade**, because even a volatile powerhouse should handle a roster held together by a $133 Devaughn Vele and a Tuesday trade for Goff — just set the lineup and walk away, commish. My Upset Alert is **Shawnee**, projected dead last and 2-0 anyway — and if she and Jack both cash, the household goes to 6-0 and Week 10, when they finally play each other, becomes a very tense dinner. And the bold one, because somebody has to watch the QB scramble: the waiver quarterbacks win the week _from the bench_.

### 🔮 Picks

- **Lock of the Week** — The People's Commissioner — the league's top scorer against a lineup built from the injury report and a $133 Vele. Don't overthink it, Wade.
- **Upset Alert** — Swift kick in the Dak — projected dead last, undefeated anyway. Set the lineup, Shawnee, and this one's yours.
- **Bold Prediction** — Jordan Love outscores Bryce Young from Wade's bench _and_ Justin Herbert outscores Matthew Stafford from Kevin's — two managers who got the QB scramble right and started the wrong guy.
