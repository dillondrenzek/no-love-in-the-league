{%- comment -%} Week navigation, shared by the week pages and the season page: a numbered pill per week of the season. Completed weeks are lightly filled, the week the league is on (weeks.yml `current`) is bold, and the week being viewed is outlined. Params: year = the season year active = the week being viewed (week pages; omit on the season page) home = true to lead with a "← Season home" link (week pages); otherwise the row is labelled "Weeks:" (season page) Special once-a-season pages (the Halfway page) join the row too: they set a fractional `week:` to sort between two weeks, a `nav_label:` for the pill, and `special: ` so their state comes from site.data[special][year]. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign year = include.year -%} {%- assign wkpages = site.pages | where: "season_year", year | where_exp: "p", "p.week" | sort: "week" -%}
