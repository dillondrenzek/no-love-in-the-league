## The Shiva

The league championship. Winning the title makes you the reigning **Shiva** until
someone takes it from you. Champions are honored on the [History]({{ '/history/' | relative_url }})
and [Owners]({{ '/teams/' | relative_url }}) pages.

## The Sacko

Last place. Finishing dead last in the final standings earns you the **Sacko** —
the mark of shame that lives forever on your owner page.

## Playoffs

- Six teams make the winners bracket, seeded by regular-season finish.
- The bracket is double-elimination.
- In six-team-bracket years, the 5th–8th place finishes are decided the
  second-to-last playoff week, so those teams' final-week games don't count for
  anything.

## Co-championship

If the championship game ends in an exact tie, the title is shared: both managers
are **co-champions** and each is credited with half a Shiva. (Happened once, in
2016 — Wade and Jono, 114.8–114.8.)
